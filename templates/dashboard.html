What do AUC, accuracy and ± mean?
AUC
How well malware probabilities rank malware above goodware. Higher is better; 1 means perfect ranking and 0.5 means chance-level ranking.
Accuracy
The share of files classified correctly at the fixed decision threshold of {{ comparison.threshold }}.
± standard deviation
Variation across the validation folds. Accuracy variation is shown in percentage points. These whiskers are not confidence intervals.
